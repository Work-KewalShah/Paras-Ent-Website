# Hindi Translations — Paras Enterprises Website

This document contains the complete Hindi translation for every key in `en.json`,
organized in the same structure. Common English/technical words (CCTV, WiFi, AI, PBX,
App, WhatsApp, Google, etc.) are kept as-is, matching how they're actually spoken in
everyday Hindi business conversation — not translated into formal/literary Hindi
equivalents nobody uses in speech.

**For Claude Code:** each `Key:` line maps directly to the matching key in `en.json`.
Insert the `Hindi:` value as the corresponding string/array in `hi.json`. Proper nouns
(company name, client names, address, phone numbers) are excluded — they don't appear in
`en.json` either, per the confirmed decision to keep them Latin-script in both languages.

## Notes on translation approach

- Common technical/business English words kept as-is (transliterated or left in Latin
  script where that's how they're actually said): CCTV, WiFi, AI, PTZ, PBX, IP, RFID,
  Bluetooth, App, WhatsApp, Google, सिक्योरिटी (security), सर्विस (service), इंस्टॉलेशन
  (installation), गारंटी (guarantee) — these are all standard in everyday spoken Hindi in
  a business context, not translated into more formal/literary Hindi equivalents.
- The `{{number}}`, `{{title}}`, and `{{year}}` placeholders are interpolation variables —
  left untouched, exactly as in the English source, since i18next fills them in at
  runtime.
- `contact.whatsappButton` is intentionally left as the English word "WhatsApp" since it's
  a brand name, not translated content.
